Day cream and night cream are not the same thing. Here is a quick guide to choosing.

## Day

A lighter texture: it absorbs quickly and prepares the skin for make-up.

## Night

A richer texture: it accompanies the skin's rest.

## And the eye contour?

The skin around the eyes is thin: it needs a dedicated product, applied by patting in gently.
